FilmBox 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 8 日

FilmBox 是一个记录想看和看过的影视作品的工具。我们尽量少碰你的数据：没有账号系统，不收集个人信息，不做广告追踪。

我们存了什么

你加入「囤片箱」的影片和观看状态（待看 / 看过），只保存在你手机本地的数据库里，不会上传到我们的服务器。删除 App 时这些数据会一起删除。

你主动使用「导出囤片箱」时，App 会生成一个备份文件，存到哪里由你在系统分享面板里决定（例如「文件」App、iCloud 云盘或聊天软件）。之后这个文件由对应的服务按它们自己的隐私政策处理。

网络请求

搜索影片、浏览榜单、查看详情时，App 会请求下面的服务。请求里只包含影片相关的参数（例如搜索关键词、影片编号），不包含你的姓名、联系方式或设备里的其他数据：

  • FilmBox 中转服务器（运行在 Cloudflare 上）：把请求转发给 TMDB 和 OMDb，并缓存一小段时间以加快速度。服务器为了防止滥用，会短时间记录请求来源 IP，不做其他用途，不和任何身份信息关联。
  • TMDB（themoviedb.org）：影片资料和海报图片。
  • OMDb（omdbapi.com）：IMDb 评分。
  • YouTube、哔哩哔哩：只在你点开预告片时加载，播放器由这些平台提供，适用它们的隐私政策。

点「去哪看」里的平台时，App 会在浏览器或对应 App 里打开该平台的搜索页，之后的行为适用该平台的隐私政策。

我们不做的事

  • 不要求注册或登录。
  • 不收集位置、通讯录、照片、广告标识符等信息。
  • 不使用第三方统计或广告 SDK。
  • 不出售或分享你的数据。

儿童

FilmBox 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的信息。

政策变更

如果这份政策有变化，我们会更新本页并修改上方的生效日期。

FilmBox Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

FilmBox is a tool for keeping track of films and shows you want to watch or have watched. It has no accounts, collects no personal information and does no ad tracking.

What is stored

The titles you add to your library and their status (want to watch / watched) are stored only in a local database on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers and are deleted when you delete the app.

When you choose "Export library", the app creates a backup file and you decide where it goes through the system share sheet (for example the Files app, iCloud Drive or a messaging app). From then on that service handles the file under its own privacy policy.

Network requests

When you search, browse charts or open a title, the app contacts the services below. Requests contain only film-related parameters (such as a search term or a title ID), never your name, contact details or other data on your device:

  • FilmBox relay server (hosted on Cloudflare): forwards requests to TMDB and OMDb and caches responses briefly. To prevent abuse it keeps the requesting IP address for a short time, uses it for nothing else and never links it to an identity.
  • TMDB (themoviedb.org): film information and poster images.
  • OMDb (omdbapi.com): IMDb ratings.
  • YouTube and Bilibili: loaded only when you open a trailer. Their players are provided by those platforms under their privacy policies.

Tapping a platform under "Where to watch" opens that platform's search page in your browser or its app, which is covered by that platform's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up or login.
  • No collection of location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or similar data.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.

Children

FilmBox is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
